Owe Log – Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Owe Log ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal finance utility that helps you track informal debts and tabs between friends. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. The app has no backend, no user accounts, and no analytics.

All data you enter — names, amounts, notes, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using your device's local storage. It never leaves your device.

2. Data Shared With Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties. There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics tools, no crash reporting services, and no third-party integrations of any kind embedded in the app.

3. Internet Access

Owe Log does not require or use an internet connection. No data is sent to or received from any external server at any time.

4. Device Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or any other sensitive device feature.

5. Children's Privacy

Owe Log is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, no special handling for children is required.

6. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app. We have no access to your data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
